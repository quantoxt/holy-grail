## The Mission

Build a multi-layered, AI-enhanced algorithmic trading system for Deriv's synthetic indices. Not a "set and forget" bot — an **Autonomous Hedge Fund** that requires oversight, continuous optimization, and intelligent risk management.

## Core Premise

Deriv synthetic indices are generated by a **Cryptographically Secure Random Number Generator (CSRNG)**. They mimic real-world volatility but are **unaffected by news, geopolitics, or economic events**. Technical analysis works because the algorithm is programmed to follow patterns — but the "House" maintains a mathematical edge long-term.

Our edge: **AI-driven regime detection + dynamic risk scaling** to exploit high-probability windows and survive everything else.

## The Three Layers

| Layer | Name | Role | Speed |
|-------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | **Soldier** (Execution Bot) | Emotionless entry/exit via fixed mathematical triggers | Millisecond |
| 2 | **Watcher** (AI Regime Detection) | Classify market regime → Kill switch for bad conditions | Seconds |
| 3 | **Sentinel** (Risk & Confidence Manager) | Monitor performance logs → Scale lot size on 90%+ confidence | Minutes |

## Key Principles

- **Signal-only execution** — no gut feelings, no manual overrides mid-trade
- **Regime-aware** — bot stops trading when conditions don't fit its strategy
- **Confidence scaling** — small lots normally, scale up only when AI says "sure situation"
- **Continuous monitoring** — not passive income, it's an active fund
